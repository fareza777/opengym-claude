### Short description (80)
```
Log gym & calisthenics workouts. Offline, no account, no ads, no subscription.
```
*(77 characters)*

### Full description

```
Tempa logs every set you lift, works out what you should lift next, and shows
you the proof that it is working.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no subscription.


● WHY TEMPA

Most gym apps lock your log behind a login on their servers, make you create an
account, then charge a monthly subscription. Tempa is the opposite: your
training log lives on your phone, you can export it whenever you like, and the
app is open source.


● IT WORKS OUT THE NEXT WEIGHT — IT DOESN'T JUST RECORD ONE

This is the part most free trackers do not do. Pick a progression rule and Tempa
does the arithmetic:

  · Linear progression — hit every rep, the weight goes up
  · Greyskull LP — a final set to failure decides the next jump
  · Double progression — climb a rep range first, then add load
  · Add time — for planks, hangs and loaded carries

Open a session and the numbers are already right on screen, and every target
tells you WHY it is that number. Missed reps never advance the load, and a stall
triggers a deload on its own.


● CALISTHENICS TREATED AS CALISTHENICS

Push-ups, pull-ups, dips and 300-odd other movements arrive knowing they carry
no load: no weight column, no working-weight prompt — one stepper, log the reps.
Reps climb instead of load. Add a dip belt and progression follows the weight
again.


● 1,324 EXERCISES WITH ANIMATIONS

Search by muscle or by the equipment you actually own. Download your plan's
exercises over wifi, then train with no signal at all.


● ALSO IN THE BOX

  · Weekly plan — one routine per weekday, easy to reschedule
  · Supersets and timed exercises (planks, hangs, farmer's walks)
  · Estimated 1RM with its own progress curve
  · Reps per side for lunges, single-arm rows and the rest
  · Muscle map — what you have not trained this week
  · A year-long activity heatmap and a weekly streak
  · Body-weight chart with a goal line
  · The screen stays awake while a workout is running
  · Rest timer with notifications
  · Optional per-set effort (RIR / RPE)
  · Dark and light themes, 9 accent colours
  · Import from FitNotes, Strong and Hevy
  · Export a JSON backup any time
  · Share your plan with a friend as one small file


● PRIVACY

No account. No analytics. No ad SDK. No tracking. The app makes exactly one kind
of network request: it downloads an exercise animation from a public CDN the
first time you open that exercise — and caches it so it is never fetched twice.
Your training log is never sent anywhere.

Google Drive auto-backup is deliberately switched off for your training data. If
you want a copy, export one yourself from Settings.


● OPEN SOURCE

Tempa is a fork of openGym by Duarte Santos, released under the AGPL v3. The
source is public:
https://github.com/fareza777/opengym-claude

Exercise data comes from hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset and remains under that
dataset's own terms.
```

## Release checklist

Run before every upload:

```bash
cd frontend && npm run check:release
```

Then:

- [ ] `npm run android:bundle` produces `app-release.aab`
- [ ] Upload key generated and **backed up somewhere you will still have in five
      years** — losing it means you cannot ship an update to this listing ever
      again. Enrol in Play App Signing.
- [ ] `versionCode` incremented since the previous upload
- [ ] Privacy policy URL reachable and public
- [ ] Data safety form filled in from `docs/PRIVACY.md` (answer: collects no data)
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire completed
- [ ] Target audience set to 18+
- [ ] Screenshots and feature graphic uploaded for both locales
- [ ] Internal testing track first, on at least one real device, before production
